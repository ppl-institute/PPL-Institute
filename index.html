Who We Are

The PPL Institute is a nonpartisan research institute specializing in social psychology and political science to understand and explain public opinion and behavior within the United States. Our research is public and accessible to both the scientific community and the general public. By making our work public to the people, we hope to address growing disparities of information access among Americans.
