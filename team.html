The Team

Ryan R. Rajpal

Primary Investigator, Founder

Ryan is a political psychologist specializing in American politics, public opinion, political communication, and social dynamics. His previous research examined how platforms like Twitter, Reddit, and TikTok influence personal well-being and political engagement. His current work investigates affective polarization and the formation of political beliefs within shared digital culture. He holds a bachelor’s degree in political science and philosophy from Rutgers University, where he graduated with the highest honors.

Paige Iino
