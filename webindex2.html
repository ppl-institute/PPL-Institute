About Us

The PPL Institute, which studies psychology and politics in the pursuit of liberty, is a non-partisan research institute with the mission of improving scientific literacy by making information accessible. We are dedicated to producing high quality research in a range of subject areas related to American politics and social psychology. Our aim, specifically, is to foster humane and data-driven conversations while building cross-partisan community.

Consulting Services

Political consulting services are offered to interested parties. In particular, we provide public opinion polling services with a guaranteed report within two weeks after the poll period closes. Please email us for more information.
