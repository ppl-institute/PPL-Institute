Who Are You Talking To? The Discernment of AI and Human Content on Social Media

This study explores whether individuals can distinguish between social media posts authored by humans vs AI-generated content. We find that participants can generally differentiate between AI and human content when images are included, but struggle when the content is soley tect based.
